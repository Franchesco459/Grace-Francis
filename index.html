Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear? +
We're going for romantic elegance with a touch of African tradition! Please dress in African attire. Think traditional casual dresses, agbadas, and your most elegant outfits. Our color palette is blush pink, gold, navy, sage green, and cream - feel free to complement these colors!
When should I RSVP by? +
Please RSVP by November 19, 2026. This helps us finalize our guest count with the venue and caterers. You can RSVP by contacting us directly or through our wedding coordinator.
Can I bring a plus-one? +
Due to venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests formally invited on your invitation. If you received a plus-one, it will be indicated on your invitation. Please reach out if you have any questions!
Will there be parking available? +
Yes! There is ample parking available at St. Mary's Catholic Church and at the reception venue. Attendants will be available to assist with parking directions.
Are children welcome? +
While we love your little ones, we've decided to make our wedding an adult-only celebration. We hope this gives you a chance to enjoy a night out and celebrate with us!
Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors? +
The ceremony will take place indoors at St. Mary's Catholic Church, and the reception will be indoors at our reception venue.
