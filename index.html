Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear? +
We're going for romantic elegance with a touch of African tradition! Please dress in African attire. Think traditional casual dresses, agbadas, and your most elegant outfits. Our colour palette is Seafoam Mist (Mint Green), Navy Blue, Champagne Gold, Blush Pink, and White - feel free to complement these colours! See the Wedding Colour Palette section above for exact colour codes.
When should I RSVP by? +
Please RSVP by November 19, 2026. This helps us finalize our guest count with the venue and caterers. You can RSVP by contacting us directly or through Our Points of Contact.
